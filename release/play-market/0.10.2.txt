What's new in 0.10.2

- Improved first launch and setup.
- Category and currency setup is now simpler.
- New users can choose starter category groups.
- Categories navigation is more reliable.
- Categories can open a new payment with the selected category.
- Russian language switching now refreshes the app screen immediately.
- Existing default accounts and standard categories can show missing Russian display names after update.
- Added small stability fixes.

Что нового в 0.10.2

- Улучшен первый запуск и настройка приложения.
